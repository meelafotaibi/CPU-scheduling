About Platform

What is AlgoVisual Hub?

AlgoVisual Hub is an interactive educational platform designed to help Computer Science students, developers, and enthusiasts master complex algorithms through visual learning. Unlike traditional static textbooks or code-only tutorials, our platform brings abstract concepts to life with real-time, step-by-step animations.

We cover a wide range of essential topics including Data Structures (DSA), sorting and searching algorithms, Operating Systems (CPU Scheduling, Memory Management), Artificial Intelligence, and Computer Graphics. Each visualizer is built to be responsive, intuitive, and educational, allowing you to input your own data and watch exactly how the algorithm processes it.

Built by passionate developers for the community, AlgoVisual Hub is completely free and open-source. Our mission is to make computer science education accessible, engaging, and effective for everyone, from university students preparing for exams to software engineers brushing up for technical interviews.

Why Choose AlgoVisual Hub?
