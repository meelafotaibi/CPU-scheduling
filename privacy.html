Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

✅ Our Commitment

AlgoVisual Hub is a completely free, ad-free educational platform. We do not sell user data, do not require account creation, and the platform will remain free forever.

1. Information We Collect

AlgoVisual Hub is a client-side educational tool. We do not require you to create an account. We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number during normal use of the platform.

If you choose to submit feedback or feature requests via our contact form, providing a name is completely optional. We do not require or collect your email address, accounts, or personal tracking details. Feedback messages are saved anonymously solely to improve the visualizers and educational content. If you submit an optional support contribution, only the payment details you voluntarily provide to our secure payment processors are used to process the transaction.

2. No Advertising

AlgoVisual Hub does not display advertisements of any kind. There are no ad networks, no advertising cookies, and no advertising identifiers on this platform. This will never change.

3. Local Storage

AlgoVisual Hub may use your browser's local storage to save preferences such as theme selection (dark/light mode) and learning progress. This data stays entirely on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

4. Optional Support Contributions

If you choose to support the platform financially, payments are processed by third-party providers (Paddle or PayPal). AlgoVisual Hub does not store your payment information. Please review the privacy policies of those providers for details on how they handle your data.

5. Third-Party Links

The platform may contain links to external educational resources. We have no control over the content or privacy practices of those sites and are not responsible for them.

6. Children's Privacy

AlgoVisual Hub is an educational platform suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no account creation is required, no personal data is collected during normal use.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us via our Contact Page
