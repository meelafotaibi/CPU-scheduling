Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

Thank you for visiting AlgoVisual Hub. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and handle information when you visit our educational website.

1. Information We Collect

This website is an educational tool. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, address, or phone number.

2. Cookies and Advertising

We use third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Log Files

Like many other websites, we may use log files. The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This information is used to analyze trends, administer the site, track user's movement around the site, and gather demographic information.
