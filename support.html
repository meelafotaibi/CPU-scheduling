❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is AlgoVisual Hub?

AlgoVisual Hub is a free educational platform that helps computer science students master algorithms through interactive visualizations. We focus on making complex concepts easy to understand through step-by-step animations.

Is it really free?

Yes! AlgoVisual Hub is completely free to use. We're supported by ads to keep the platform running and accessible to everyone.

Which algorithms are available?

Currently, we have 8 working visualizers: 3 sorting algorithms (Bubble, Selection, Insertion) and 5 operating system algorithms (CPU Scheduling, Memory Allocation, Page Replacement, Banker's Algorithm, Disk Scheduling). Check our Roadmap for upcoming features!

Can I use custom inputs?

Yes! Most visualizers allow you to input your own data to see how the algorithm behaves with different scenarios.

How do I report a bug?

Please email us at devmeelo4@gmail.com with details about the issue, including which algorithm you were using and what went wrong.

Can I contribute or suggest features?

Absolutely! We welcome feedback and feature requests. Send us an email with your ideas!
